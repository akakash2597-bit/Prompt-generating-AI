The rulebook

Every prompt Pro Prompt AI generates draws from this ladder — basic habits first, professional-grade techniques layered on top.

Foundation — fix these first
01

Be specific

Replace "write about X" with exactly what, for whom, and how long.

02

Give it a role

"Act as a senior copy editor" narrows vocabulary, judgment, and tone.

03

State the format

Bullets, table, JSON, word count — say the shape you want back.

04

Add context

Audience, purpose, and constraints the model can't guess on its own.

Professional — for consistent, repeatable output
05

Few-shot examples

One or two sample input/output pairs anchor tone and structure.

06

Chain-of-thought

"Think step by step before answering" for anything with reasoning.

07

Explicit constraints

Say what to avoid, not just what to include — length caps, banned phrases.

08

Success criteria

Define what a "good" answer looks like so the model can self-check.

09

Delimiters

Wrap reference text in tags or triple-quotes so instructions and data don't blur.

10

Iterative refinement

Ask for a draft, then a critique pass, then a final version — in one prompt.
